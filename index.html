PILA 2026
Personal Intelligence in the Agentic AI Era

KDD 2026 · 9–13 August · Jeju, Republic of Korea · Co-located workshop

About the Workshop

If you work on personalized agents, user modeling, or human-centered AI, we hope you will consider joining our call for papers. Co-located with ACM SIGKDD KDD 2026, PILA ’26 (Personal Intelligence in the Agentic AI Era) is the first dedicated workshop on this theme. We ask how we can move from general-purpose models toward systems that explicitly model individual users and adapt through memory, interaction, and continual learning—rather than static, one-size-fits-all behavior.

We welcome discussion of user modeling and personalized alignment; self-evolving memory and lifelong adaptation; benchmarks, datasets, and evaluation; real deployment settings and applications; and privacy, safety, and trustworthiness for user-adaptive systems. The program will combine invited talks, contributed orals and posters, and a panel. The workshop is in person on the morning of Sunday, 9 August 2026 on Jeju Island, Republic of Korea, alongside the KDD main conference—a good setting for focused exchange. We sincerely invite submissions and encourage KDD authors in personalization-related areas to use PILA to showcase and discuss their work.

Call for Papers

The CFP is open. We welcome you to submit—and to share this announcement with colleagues who may be interested.

Topics we welcome

We invite submissions on (but are not limited to) the following directions:

(1) Personalized agent understanding and user modeling

How personal AI agents build robust user models from preferences, behavior, and evolving context: profile alignment, preference drift, uncertainty-aware personalization, and benchmark design for long-horizon user understanding.

(2) Agent memory, retrieval, and knowledge integration

Memory architectures and retrieval strategies for personal intelligence: episodic and semantic memory, context compression, and hybrid retrieval–generation approaches that support reliable long-term agent behavior.

(3) Adaptive planning and tool use for personal agents

Tool routing, multi-agent collaboration, self-correction loops, and adaptive control policies for dynamic, real-world tasks grounded in individual users.

(4) Evaluation, alignment, and safety in personal intelligence

User-aligned objectives, controllability, transparency, and evaluation protocols that better reflect real user outcomes—not proxy metrics alone.

(5) Privacy, deployment, and human–AI interaction

Privacy-preserving personalization; on-device and federated approaches; interaction design for human–agent collaboration; and lessons from deploying personal AI in production.

Submission information

Dates. Workshop site and CFP: 1 April 2026 (AOE). Paper submission: 21 May 2026 (AOE). Notification: 10 June 2026 (AOE). Camera-ready: 22 June 2026 (AOE). All deadlines are 23:59 Anywhere on Earth (AOE) on the stated date.

Format. Submissions are in English, use the ACM two-column conference proceedings layout, and follow the official ACM proceedings templates or Overleaf (ACM SIG Proceedings). Full research papers are 4–8 pages, excluding references and appendices. For review, upload one anonymized PDF. For LaTeX, anonymized review is easiest with: 

\documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart}

Double-blind review. Omit author names, affiliations, and identifying acknowledgments; anonymize hyperlinks and any supplementary material shown to reviewers. After acceptance, the camera-ready PDF may include full author metadata.

You may attach optional proofs, pseudocode, code, or data descriptions (no fixed page limit; single- or double-column is fine). Reviewers are not required to read supplements; the main paper must be self-contained.

OpenReview. This workshop uses OpenReview for submission and peer review—please use only the PILA group: https://openreview.net/group?id=KDD.org/2026/Workshop/PILA. Sign in (or register), create a submission under that group, and upload your PDF. Do not submit by email.

Accepted papers are non-archival: camera-ready versions appear on this site only and are not part of the ACM KDD 2026 main proceedings. Acceptance at PILA does not prevent you from later submitting the same work to other conferences or journals. We will award a Best Paper Award to the most promising innovative contribution among accepted papers.

We look forward to your submissions and to meeting you in Jeju.
