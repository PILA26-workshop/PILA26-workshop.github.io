PILA 2026
Personal Intelligence in the Agentic AI Era

KDD 2026 · 9–13 August · Jeju, Republic of Korea · Co-located workshop

About

PILA 2026 targets personal intelligence: LLM- and agent-based systems that model individual users and adapt via memory, interaction, and continual learning—beyond aggregate or one-size-fits-all objectives. Coverage spans user modeling, memory and retrieval, planning and tools, evaluation and alignment, privacy and deployment. Format: invited talks, contributed talks/posters, panel. Venue: in person, co-located with KDD 2026.

Deadlines: 23:59 AOE on the listed date.



Submission format

English; ACM two-column proceedings style — ACM templates, Overleaf. Length: 4–8 pages excluding references and optional appendices. PDF via OpenReview.

LaTeX example:

\documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart}

Double-blind: no names, affiliations, or acknowledgments that identify authors; anonymize links and supplements seen by reviewers. Camera-ready after acceptance may include full metadata.

Supplement (optional): extra proofs/code/data description—no page limit; reviewers may ignore it; main paper must be self-contained.

Submission

OpenReview (double-blind). Accepted papers: non-archival; on this site only — not in the ACM KDD 2026 main proceedings.

Best paper award

One award (program committee).

Topics

(1) User modeling & personalization

Preferences, context, drift, evaluation.

(2) Memory & retrieval

Episodic/semantic memory, RAG, long-horizon context.

(3) Planning & tool use

Tools, multi-agent, control, open-ended tasks.

(4) Evaluation, alignment, safety

Metrics, robustness, user-aligned objectives.

(5) Privacy, deployment, HCI

On-device/federated, interaction, production.
