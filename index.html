PILA 2026
Personal Intelligence in the Agentic AI Era

KDD 2026 · 9–13 August · Jeju, Republic of Korea · Co-located workshop

About the Workshop

Large language models and agent-based systems are widely deployed in user-facing products, yet many systems are still trained and evaluated for population-level goals. PILA 2026 focuses on personal intelligence: building models and agents that represent individual users and adapt inference, behavior, and decisions over time—using memory, interaction feedback, and continual learning rather than static one-size-fits-all policies.

We welcome researchers and practitioners across data mining, ML for natural language and information retrieval, human-centered computing, and trustworthy AI. Themes include user modeling and personalized alignment; memory, retrieval, and lifelong adaptation; benchmarks and evaluation; assistants, health-oriented settings, and other applications; privacy, safety, and reliability for user-adaptive systems. The program combines invited talks, contributed oral presentations and posters, and a panel discussion. The workshop is in person, co-located with ACM SIGKDD KDD 2026.

Submission format

Manuscripts must be in English and use the ACM two-column conference proceedings layout. Official templates: ACM proceedings templates, Overleaf (ACM SIG Proceedings).

The paper body is 4–8 pages, excluding references and optional appendices. For review, upload one PDF that satisfies the double-blind rules below.

LaTeX

Recommended acmart options for anonymized review:

\documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart}

Double-blind review

Omit author names, affiliations, and identifying acknowledgments.

Anonymize hyperlinks and any supplementary material shown to reviewers.

After acceptance, the camera-ready PDF may include full author metadata.

Optional supplement

You may attach proofs, pseudocode, code, or data descriptions. There is no fixed page limit; layout may be single- or double-column.

Reviewers are not required to consult supplemental material.

The main paper must be self-contained.

Submission

Use only the PILA group on OpenReview: openreview.net/group?id=KDD.org/2026/Workshop/PILA.

Sign in (or register) on OpenReview, create a submission under that group, and upload your anonymized PDF. Do not submit by email.

Review is double-blind; prepare the PDF and any supplement accordingly.

Accepted papers are non-archival. Camera-ready versions appear on this website only and are not included in the ACM KDD 2026 main conference proceedings.

Best paper award

The program committee may select one Best Paper from accepted submissions.

Topics of interest

Representative areas include, but are not limited to, the following:

(1) Personalized agent understanding and user modeling

How personal agents build and maintain user models from preferences, behavior, and evolving context: profile grounding, preference drift, uncertainty-aware personalization, and benchmark design for long-horizon user understanding.

(2) Agent memory, retrieval, and knowledge integration

Memory architectures and retrieval for personal intelligence: episodic and semantic memory, context compression, retrieval-augmented generation, and hybrid retrieval–generation pipelines for reliable long-term agent behavior.

(3) Adaptive planning and tool use for personal agents

Reasoning and action under user goals and constraints: tool routing and selection, multi-agent collaboration, self-correction, and adaptive policies for dynamic or open-ended tasks.

(4) Evaluation, alignment, and safety in personal intelligence

Trustworthy personal AI: objectives aligned with users, controllability and transparency, robustness under distribution shift, and evaluation protocols tied to real user outcomes rather than proxy metrics alone.

(5) Privacy, deployment, and human–AI interaction

Privacy-preserving personalization; on-device and federated approaches; interaction design for human–agent collaboration; and deployment experience with personal AI in production settings.
